Station *keys* are those positions rounded to 1 decimal, as strings, while

## Station values are not always scalars

At a discontinuity a station holds a **2-element `[left, right]` pair** - the value
either side of the step - instead of a float. Across this solve 2254 station values
are pairs (190-261 per combination), always on 6 of the 16 members: those with
`member_discontinuities > 0`. Combination `10` has none at all, so never feature-
detect on a single combination. A quantity that stays continuous through the step
keeps a scalar at that same station, so this varies per result key, not just per
station - member `1` of combination `11` at station `33.3`:

```
axial_force       29.2=-0.001   33.3=[-0.001, 0.002]   37.5=0.002
shear_force_y     29.2=-0.033   33.3=[-0.042, 0.035]   37.5=0.026
shear_force_z     29.2=-0.001   33.3=[-0.001, 0]   37.5=0
bending_moment_y  29.2=0   33.3=0   37.5=0
bending_moment_z  29.2=-0.001   33.3=-0.003   37.5=-0.001
torsion           29.2=0   33.3=0   37.5=0
```

So `Math.max(...Object.values(series))`, or any arithmetic run straight over a
station series, will break. Flatten the pairs first, or read `member_minimums`
/ `member_maximums`, which are always scalars.
